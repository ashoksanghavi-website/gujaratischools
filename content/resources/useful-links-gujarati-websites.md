A curated collection of **useful Gujarati websites** for families and teachers, including:

- Gujarati **newspapers and publications**
- **Literature** and poetry (kavita, gazals)
- **Grammar** references and **dictionaries**
- **Songs, poems and children's stories**
- Resources **about Gujarat** itself

These links help children hear, read and enjoy Gujarati beyond the classroom. If you have a favourite resource to add, [let us know](/contact).
