Use this form for a **Gujarati school, temple or community group** to join CGS as an organisation.

## How to join

1. Download and open the fillable PDF form.
2. Complete your organisation's details and **save the file with a new name**.
3. Email the completed form to **Vijyaben Bhanderi** at [vijya.bhanderi@gujaratischools.org](mailto:vijya.bhanderi@gujaratischools.org).
4. Pay the subscription online where possible.

New organisations should also complete the [School Information Form](/resources/school-information-form) so we can support you.
