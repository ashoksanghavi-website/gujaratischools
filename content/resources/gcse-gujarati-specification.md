The current **Pearson Edexcel GCSE (9-1) Gujarati** specification is available directly from Pearson. In 2021 Pearson made two small corrections: updated Paper 2 guidance on submitting speaking-assessment recordings via the Learner Work Transfer portal (page 14), and minor corrections to the Appendix 3 vocabulary list (page 76).

Always download the latest version from Pearson so you are working from the corrected vocabulary list. See our [news update](/news/pearson-gcse-specification-update) for details.
