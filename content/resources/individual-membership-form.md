Use this form to join the Consortium of Gujarati Schools as an **individual**, a teacher, parent or supporter of Gujarati education.

## How to join

1. Download and open the form (it is a fillable PDF).
2. Complete your details and **save the file with a new name**.
3. Email the completed form to **Vijyaben Bhanderi** at [vijya.bhanderi@gujaratischools.org](mailto:vijya.bhanderi@gujaratischools.org).
4. Pay the subscription online where possible.

See the [Membership page](/membership) for full details on benefits and payment.
