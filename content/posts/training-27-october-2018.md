On **27 October 2018**, CGS ran a teacher training session in **Wembley**. The day supported volunteer teachers, many of them parents, in delivering high-quality Gujarati lessons and preparing students for formal qualifications.

## What teachers took away

- Practical lesson-planning tools for Gujarati.
- Strategies for teaching reading and writing in Gujarati script.
- Shared resources and ongoing support from the CGS network.
