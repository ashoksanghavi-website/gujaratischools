On **28 November 2020**, CGS ran a teacher training session focused on the **Speaking Endorsement** for Gujarati. Teachers explored how to prepare and assess students' speaking, and how to build confidence in spoken Gujarati throughout the course.

## What was covered

- Structuring the Speaking Endorsement across the year.
- Preparing students for the speaking assessment.
- Practical classroom activities to build spoken fluency.

## Course material

The **Speaking Endorsement course material** from this session is available in the [Resources library](/resources?type=Training+Materials) for teachers to download and use with their own classes.
