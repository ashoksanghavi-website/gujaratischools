We are pleased to announce the **CGS committee for 2024–2026**. Our volunteers give their time to support Gujarati schools, teachers and students across the UK.

The committee is chaired by **Jayant Tanna**, with **Vijyaben Bhanderi** leading on membership. Together the committee oversees training, resources, exam liaison and the running of the charity.

You can see the full committee, along with an archive of past committees, on the [Committee page](/committee).

If you would like to get involved or support the work of CGS, please [contact us](/contact) or [become a member](/membership).
