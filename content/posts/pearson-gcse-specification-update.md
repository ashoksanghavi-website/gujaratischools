Pearson has made **two small changes** to the GCSE Gujarati specification. Neither changes what is taught, but teachers should be aware of both.

## The two changes

| Section | Page | What changed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paper 2, assessment information | 14 | Amended with updated guidance on submitting speaking assessment recordings via the online **Learner Work Transfer** portal. |
| Appendix 3, vocabulary list | 76 | Updated with minor corrections. |

## What teachers need to do

- Familiarise yourself with the **Learner Work Transfer** portal ahead of the speaking assessment window.
- Download the latest specification from the [Resources library](/resources?exam=GCSE) so you are working from the corrected vocabulary list.

These are minor, administrative updates, but getting the speaking-recording submission right matters, so please read the Paper 2 guidance carefully.
