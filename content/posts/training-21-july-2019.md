On **21 July 2019**, CGS held a teacher training day at the **Oshwal Ekta Centre**. Teachers from Gujarati schools across the region came together for a day of professional development, sharing practice and building confidence in the classroom.

## Themes of the day

- Effective teaching approaches for mixed-ability Gujarati classes.
- Preparing students for GCSE and A-Level.
- Building a supportive community of Gujarati teachers.
