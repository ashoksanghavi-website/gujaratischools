Welcome to the **Consortium of Gujarati Schools**. Our first e-Newsletter, from **April 2020**, introduced CGS to members and set out how we support Gujarati teaching and learning across the UK.

## About CGS

CGS trains parent and volunteer teachers so their students can achieve in Gujarati GCSE and A-Level exams. We provide learning resources, run training courses, and bring Gujarati schools together as a community.

We are delighted to have you with us. To stay in touch, [join our mailing list](https://eepurl.com/gSEMzn) and explore the [Resources library](/resources).
