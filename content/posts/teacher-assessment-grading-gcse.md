CGS held an important training session on **Teacher Assessment Grading for GCSE Gujarati** on **20 April 2021**. With exams affected by the pandemic, teachers were asked to submit assessment grades, and this session explained the process clearly.

## Watch the training

The full session is available to watch on the [CGS YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCUWtYiPxTcErR4G7gAQhk9g). It walks teachers through gathering evidence, applying standards fairly and submitting grades with confidence.

## Key points covered

- What counts as valid assessment evidence for Gujarati.
- Applying grade standards consistently across students.
- The submission process and deadlines.
