# Today's update — what changed, and where to see it

Live site: **https://gujaratischools.vercel.app**
(Best viewed on a phone — that's where most of your teachers and parents are.)

Each item below is something you raised, what we did about it, and the exact page
to look at.

---

### 1. The home page was too empty, with big gaps
We tightened the spacing across every section so the page reads as one confident
whole, not a stack of boxes with holes between them.
**See it:** https://gujaratischools.vercel.app/

### 2. A clear "students and teachers" function, right under the hero
The home page now leads with two big doors — **Learn Gujarati** (find a school)
and **Teach Gujarati** (we train you) — so a new family or a would-be teacher
knows what to do in the first three seconds.
**See it:** https://gujaratischools.vercel.app/

### 3. Find a school — the tool that brings in new students
A real, searchable directory of the Gujarati schools across the UK. A parent
finds their nearest class by town, and **every school has an "Enquire" button**
that opens a short form and sends the enquiry straight to CGS — no phone call,
no hunting around.
**See it:** https://gujaratischools.vercel.app/find-a-school

### 4. Find a school — made premium, and no more empty space
Redesigned into one dense, tidy grid with colour-coded regions, location pins and
a "schools · towns · regions" summary at the top, so it always looks like a full,
living network.
**See it:** https://gujaratischools.vercel.app/find-a-school

### 5. Become a teacher — easy to say yes to
A warm page that says plainly "you don't need to be qualified, we train you," and
lets a volunteer **register their interest in under a minute** through a short
form.
**See it:** https://gujaratischools.vercel.app/teach

### 6. Membership — two easy ways to join, and a fast promise
Each form now offers **Apply online** (a short pop-up form you fill and send) or
**Download the form** as before. Online applications reach CGS straight away, and
the site tells applicants they'll hear back within 12 hours.
**See it:** https://gujaratischools.vercel.app/membership

### 7. Membership steps — fixed a display issue
The "How to join" steps were cutting off the secretary's email; that is now clean
and readable, with the address shown clearly below.
**See it:** https://gujaratischools.vercel.app/membership

### 8. The pop-up forms now work perfectly on a phone
The online forms open in a tidy pop-up that fits any screen and scrolls smoothly,
so every field and the send button are always reachable.
**See it:** https://gujaratischools.vercel.app/membership (tap "Apply online")

### 9. Tidier navigation and cleaner writing throughout
The top menu no longer crowds or cuts off on any screen size, and the wording
across the whole site has been cleaned up to read more naturally.
**See it:** every page, e.g. https://gujaratischools.vercel.app/resources

---

**In short:** everything we changed today points at the same goal — making it
easy for new students and new teachers to find CGS and take the first step,
online, on a phone, in seconds.
