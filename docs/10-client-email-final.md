**Subject:** The new CGS website — built to help win back students and teachers

Dear Jayant,

Thank you for being so honest about where the committee stands. You are weighing
a fair question: with student and teacher numbers falling, is a website really
worth the investment? That is the exact question I set out to answer. So rather
than simply make the old site look nicer, I have rebuilt it to do one job — to
help bring in **new students and new teachers**.

It is ready for you to see now, and I would genuinely value you clicking around
before the committee decides:

**https://gujaratischools.vercel.app**

A few things it now does that the old site could not:

- **Families can find a class.** A parent searches their town and finds their
  nearest Gujarati school in seconds, then sends an enquiry with one tap that
  comes straight to CGS. → https://gujaratischools.vercel.app/find-a-school
- **New teachers can step forward.** The site says plainly what is true — you do
  not need to be qualified, we train you — and lets a willing volunteer register
  in under a minute. → https://gujaratischools.vercel.app/teach
- **Joining is effortless.** People can apply for membership online in a short
  form, or download it as before, and the site invites them to expect a reply
  within 12 hours. → https://gujaratischools.vercel.app/membership
- **Teachers can finally find the resources** — every past paper, form and
  training pack, searchable in a couple of taps on a phone.
  → https://gujaratischools.vercel.app/resources

**A word on being found online.** The groundwork that helps Google understand and
list the site is now in place, and it is fast and built for phones. But the old
site was hard to find for the searches that matter and looked dated — so when a
parent searches "Gujarati classes near me," or someone wants to help teach, they
often land on a competitor's page, and that is who gains the student or the
teacher. Nobody finds a class, or a way to help, by asking around in person any
more; they search, and they judge in seconds by how trustworthy a page looks and
how easily they can act. That is where credibility is won today, and it is what
this rebuild is made to win.

Two reassurances: nothing on the site has been invented — every school, document
and detail comes from CGS's own records — and the committee can keep it updated
yourselves, with no developer needed.

My honest view is that the website is now the cheapest and hardest-working way
CGS has to reach new families and new teachers, working around the clock at no
ongoing cost. There is no risk in saying yes: it is already built. Once the
committee is happy, the final step is simply to point gujaratischools.org to the
new site.

I would be glad to walk the committee through it on a quick call whenever suits.

Warm regards,
Prince
