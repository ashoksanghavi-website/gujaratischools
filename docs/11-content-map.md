# CGS website — where your material now lives

**Live site:** https://gujaratischools-dun.vercel.app

Everything you sent has been added to the website. It is **not** laid out as the
same folders you sent — instead every document lives in one searchable
**Resources** library that you filter (by who it's for, what type, and which
exam), and all photographs live in the **Gallery**, grouped by event. So nothing
is missing; it is simply organised for visitors to find quickly.

**Totals now live:** ~600 documents (PDFs, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, audio and
video) and ~140 photographs. Every document shows its file size before you tap it.

---

## The quickest way to find anything
- **All documents:** https://gujaratischools-dun.vercel.app/resources
  Use the filters on the left, or the search box. For example:
  - Governance (rules, minutes, reports, accounts): `/resources?type=Governance`
  - Past papers: `/resources?type=Past+Papers`
  - Training materials: `/resources?type=Training+Materials`
  - Teaching materials: `/resources?type=Teaching+Materials`
  - Exam specifications: `/resources?type=Exam+Specifications`
  - Exam information / updates: `/resources?type=Exam+Information`
  - Membership & school forms: `/resources?type=Membership+Forms`
  - For teachers / leaders / parents: `/resources?audience=Teachers` (or `Leaders`, `Parents`)
  - GCSE only / A Level only: `/resources?exam=GCSE` (or `A+Level`)

## A few things handled specially
- **Membership & school forms:** the new **2026–2028** membership forms and the
  **Sept 2027–28** School Information Form are the current ones on the
  [Membership page](https://gujaratischools-dun.vercel.app/membership). Earlier
  versions (2018/2019/2020) are also kept in Resources, labelled with their year.
- **One large listening file** (a 180 MB GCSE sample WAV) was **converted to MP3**
  (32 MB) so it plays and downloads on the site — the content is unchanged.
- **Not added (on purpose):** exact duplicate "- Copy" files, and the saved
  *email web-page* files and the *PDF print-out of the old website* (these were
  web-page scaffolding, not documents). The real documents inside those folders
  were added.
- **Photographs:** identical/duplicate copies were removed so each photo appears
  once; all unique photos are in the Gallery.
